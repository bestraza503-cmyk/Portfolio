About Me

An operator first,
a consultant second.

I'm Muhammad Ali Raza — an eCommerce Management Specialist with almost two years of hands-on, revenue-facing experience running seller accounts across the world's biggest marketplaces. I didn't learn this from a course; I learned it from live listings, real ad budgets, suspension appeals, peak seasons and the daily grind of keeping accounts healthy.

My work sits at the intersection of three things: getting products found (research + keyword-driven listing SEO), getting them sold (PPC, pricing, promotions, buy-box strategy) and keeping the business safe (account health, policy compliance, inventory and fulfilment). That combination is what separates a store that just exists from one that compounds month after month.

I work with brand owners, private-label sellers and dropshipping operators who want a reliable pair of hands — someone who treats their account like their own business, communicates clearly, and reports on numbers that actually matter.
